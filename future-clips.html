Frequently asked

Do carousels actually pay?+

TikTok's Creator Rewards doesn't pay for photo mode, but brand campaigns do — per verified view, on whatever format the campaign allows. We show you which campaigns allow carousels before you start.

Are the examples on this page your output?+

No. Those are real posts by creators in each niche, shown with their permission, so you can see the format that's already earning. Our own output goes up the moment the first campaigns run.

Where do the hooks come from?+

A library of opening lines grouped by niche, built from what's already working in clip campaigns. Edit any of them, or write your own and we'll lay it out.

Is this AI slop?+

We assemble. Layout, typography and your footage are deterministic. AI touches at most one element, and you can switch it off entirely.

What does the founding price mean?+

$20/mo instead of $29, and it doesn't rise while you stay subscribed. No card now, nothing charged until launch.
