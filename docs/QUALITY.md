## Rubric (inherited; change it only with a written reason)

Score each round out of 100 against the closest reference set, never against the previous round: photo realism 30, specificity of copy 20, hook 15, type treatment 10, consistency of person or world 10, platform-nativeness 10, compliance 5 (must be 5 or the round is void). Measure with sharp: type height in px, visible stroke in px and as a fraction of the em, block position as a fraction of canvas height, mean luminance, contrast std, saturation, flat-area noise (reference gym family: luminance 87, contrast std 60, saturation 0.26, flat-area noise 1.34, 60 percent of pixels flat).

## Taxonomy (to write, per family and per reference set)

Niche, aspect, slide count, hook pattern (counted), text position, type size relative to canvas, stroke, what the photo is, lighting, colour, how "amateur", caption voice, emoji use, how the app is shown. For Grow with Alex: loud and quiet font roles, hierarchy per slide, density, grid, colour system, what a Level 1/2/3 progression does structurally.
