# TARGETS.md — the carousels I think you mean

Found by searching Instagram across two unrelated niches (design/marketing and
money/mindset) rather than following one account's network. The same handful of
patterns come back in both, which is what makes me think they are the format
rather than one creator's taste.

---

## The four patterns that actually recur

### A — Full-bleed cinematic photo + big type over it  ← **I think this is the one**

- *"10 Elegant Things Money Can't Buy"* — a man and a horse, dark cinematic
  light, elegant serif in white, tiny byline
- *"HOW TO DESIGN A MINIMAL Carousel"* — a person at a desk in low light, lime
  green serif headline over the photo
- *"The Carousel Framework"* — a woman outdoors, white bold sans plus an italic
  script line
- *"THE HARDEST PART OF MAKING CONTENT ISN'T posting…"* — a guy on a sofa, white
  condensed caps with an orange italic script accent
- *"CLAUDE + CANVA WORKFLOW"* — orange-graded forest, white serif/sans mix

**Why I think this is the target:** it is the most common by a distance, it
works in every niche, the photograph does the emotional work, and the type does
the selling. It is also the pattern my depth-poster family was already reaching
for and getting wrong for a fixable reason — I had stock strangers in polo
shirts where these have atmosphere and a real subject.

### B — Cutout on a light ground + one accent

- *"Time Value of Money!"* — white ground, blue bold sans, B&W DiCaprio cutout
  holding a dollar
- papanca's *"if you want to get jacked and run, use this split"* — the 193k post

Clean, cheap to produce, and the thing I have most of the machinery for already.

### C — Object collage

- papanca's *"The art of athlete maxxing"* (45.6K)
- *"4 Photos Rich Peoples don't want you to see"* — cream grid ground, fist of
  cash, gold coin bags, fanned notes, orange and black type

Rare, high effort, high save-rate. Not where I would start.

### D — Raw photo + handwritten annotation

- *"The Psychology of Money"* — an actual hand holding the actual book, with
  handwritten-looking arrows and notes over it

Deliberately unpolished. Matches the research finding that candid beats
produced. Cheapest of all to make and probably underrated.

---

## What is true in every single one

1. **A real photograph.** Not an illustration, not a vector, not a diagram.
   Every example above has a photographic image doing the emotional work.
2. **Exactly one accent colour.** Lime green and orange dominate right now. It
   lands on one or two words, never more.
3. **Type mixes faces inside one headline** — bold sans plus an italic
   serif/script accent is the single most repeated move.
4. **A person is usually in frame**, and often it is the creator themself.
5. **The type is large and sits ON the image**, not beside it in a clean panel.

---

## Where this leaves my build

The **object constellation** I built is pattern C — the rarest and hardest of
the four, and the one I chose first. That was a bad call. It is also the one
most damaged by drawn assets, because a collage is nothing but its objects.

**Pattern A is what I should be building**, and the gap is narrower than it
looks: I already have the compositor, the fit contract, the safe areas and the
rules validator. What I do not have is *one good photograph per slide with
atmosphere* — and that is now generatable, since a full-bleed background needs
no matting at all, which removes the entire failure class that has been
producing halos and blown cutouts.

Pattern D is worth a cheap experiment for the same reason — it needs one real
photo and some hand-drawn marks, no cutouts, no collage.
