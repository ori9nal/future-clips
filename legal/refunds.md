# Refund Policy

**Effective date: 12 September 2026**

## 1. Free trial

New accounts receive 2 free Slides so you can try the Services before purchase. No payment details are required.

## 2. Subscriptions — 14 day guarantee

If you are not satisfied with your first paid subscription, contact us within 14 days of the initial charge and we will refund it in full.

Renewal charges after the initial period are not refundable. You can cancel at any time from your account settings to prevent the next charge. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period and you keep access until then.

## 3. Annual plans

Annual plans can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase. After 14 days we may, at our discretion, issue a pro-rata refund for unused complete months.

## 4. Slide packs

Purchased Slide credits are non-refundable once any part of the pack has been used, because generation costs are incurred at the point of use. Wholly unused packs can be refunded within 14 days of purchase.

## 5. Failed generations

If generation fails due to a technical fault on our side, the affected Slides return to your balance automatically. You do not need to request a refund.

## 6. Duplicate or unauthorised charges

Contact us and we will investigate promptly. Confirmed duplicate or unauthorised charges are refunded in full.

## 7. Terminated accounts

Accounts terminated for breaching the Acceptable Use Policy are not eligible for refunds.

## 8. How to request a refund

Email **team@thefutureclips.com** from the address on your account, including your order reference. We respond within 3 business days. Approved refunds return to the original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days depending on your provider.

## 9. Before you dispute a charge

If something is wrong, please contact us first. We would much rather fix it directly than have you file a chargeback, and we handle legitimate requests quickly.
