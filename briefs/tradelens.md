The Product
The TradeLens App is an AI chart analysis app. Upload a screenshot of any chart and get a full technical analysis in seconds: trend, patterns, support and resistance, a clear Long / Short / Hold signal, entry, stop-loss and target, plus sentiment from 100+ news articles on that asset. It also works as a personal AI trading mentor. Crypto, stocks, forex and commodities. iOS.
