Frequently asked

What counts as one slide?+

One generated image. A typical carousel runs 6–10 slides, so 200 slides is roughly 25 carousels a month. Text edits and re-exports are free — you only pay when an image is generated.

Can I use my whole month in one day?+

Yes. There's no daily cap — batch a whole month of carousels in one sitting if that's how you work.

Do cheaper plans get worse images?+

No. Every plan generates the same quality with the same characters, realistic or illustrated. Plans differ on how many slides you get and which extras are unlocked, never on output quality.

Why pick Pro over Clipper?+

2.5x the slides, unlimited saved characters instead of one, plus view tracking so you can see which carousels actually performed and make more like them.

How does the character stay the same?+

You set a character and style once, and we hold it as a reference for every generation after. Slide eight looks like slide one, and next week's post looks like this week's.

Will anyone see it was made with AI?+

Exports come out clean — no visible watermark and no stamped badge. Nothing to crop out before you post.

What if I run out mid-month?+

Buy a slide pack and keep going — from $8 for 100. Purchased slides never expire, so anything left over is still there next month.

Can I cancel, and do you do refunds?+

Cancel any time from your settings — it stops the next charge and you keep everything you've paid for until the period ends. We don't do refunds, though: a slide costs us money at the moment it's generated and that can't be taken back. Billing mistakes are a different thing and we fix those. It's all written out in the Refund Policy.
