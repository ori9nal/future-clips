Why he is the same man on every frame

Character consistency is what makes this format work and the hardest thing to fake with an image model — a fixed seed freezes the pose, a varying seed drifts the face. It is not faked here. These are six frames from a single studio session by one photographer, so the light, the backdrop and the face are identical across the set because they were shot that way. The palette is sampled from the photographs rather than chosen. The one accent is the specular highlight measured off lit skin — a warm stone, because that is the colour actually in the frame.

What decides where the words go

  • Negative-space solverCandidate boxes are scored on mean brightness plus p95 — a box that is 90% black with one lit forearm through it scores well on the mean and reads terribly, so the p95 is weighted heavily. Type is then laid into the emptiest box the copy actually fits, falling through in score order.
  • Alternation penaltyA box pays a penalty for being chosen two slides running, so the set alternates sides instead of parking every headline in the same corner.
  • Derived scrimDarkening strength is computed from what is behind the words. A full-width band gets a band-shaped scrim, not a radial one — the radial fell off before the end of the line and left the last words on a lit shoulder.
  • Headline floorA box that can only fit the headline by shrinking it under 66px is rejected. A headline squeezed to fit any gap is not a headline.
  • Measured word-spacingThe extra-condensed black has a very narrow space glyph — "TOP SET" closed up into one word. Word-spacing fixes it and the fitter reserves 5% for the width it adds.
  • Derived countsThe hook says three signals and the closer says all three because both read SIGNALS.length. Adding a fourth signal rewrites both lines.
